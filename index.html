Good to know

Questions.

Does PortorTV come with channels?

No. PortorTV is a player only. Add a playlist from your own TV provider: an M3U link, an Xtream Codes login or a Stalker portal.

Is it free?

Yes, the free version plays one playlist with the full TV guide. Premium adds the sports hub, multiview, more playlists, movies and series, recording and more.

Which devices does it work on?

Android TV and Google TV boxes and TVs, Fire TV devices running Fire OS, and Android phones and tablets (Android 7.0 or newer).

Where can I get help or suggest a feature?

In the app go to Settings › Help & guide, join us on r/PortorTV, or email PortorDevs@gmail.com.
