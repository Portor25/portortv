PortorTV Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

PortorTV ("the app") is made by PortorDevs. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: we don't collect it.

What the app stores

To work, the app stores the following only on your device:

  • Playlist details you enter (playlist or EPG links, server addresses, usernames, passwords, MAC addresses). Passwords are kept in encrypted storage.
  • Downloaded channel lists, TV guide data, artwork and your watch history.
  • Your settings, favourites, reminders, category choices and parental-control PIN.
  • Network-folder details for recordings (address, share, username and password), if you set them up.
  • Recordings you make, saved where you choose (device, USB drive or your own network folder).

What is sent, and to whom

  • Your TV provider: the app connects to the playlist, guide and stream addresses you entered, and sends them the login details you entered, so it can download channels and play them. That provider's own privacy policy applies to that connection.
  • Your network folder: if you record to a PC, Mac or NAS, the app connects to it with the details you entered.
  • Google Play: if you buy Premium, the purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. We don't see or store your payment details.

PortorDevs does not receive, collect, sell or share any of your personal data. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Content

PortorTV is a player only and does not provide any channels or content. All content comes from the provider you choose.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Parents can lock categories with a PIN in Settings › Parental control.

Deleting your data

Remove a playlist in Settings › Playlists to delete its data, or uninstall the app to delete everything it stored. Recordings saved on a USB drive or network folder stay there until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
